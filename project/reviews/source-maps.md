# Six conclusions on price – logic (conclusions)

1
Price does not predict sales
2
Discount depth runs opposite to margin
Perfume brands from Rs 449 to Rs 799 sell $9M to $54M. The cheapest (Bella Vita) is the largest; the dearest at full price (Bombay Shaving) grows fastest.
Brands near list are profitable: Reginald 25%, Muuchstac 37%, Gillette 31%. TMC discounts deepest and loses most. Bella Vita is the exception: its fixed discount is the brand idea.

3
Brand power shows as engagement and ratings, not followers
4
A premium is paid for a specific result, never for a general identity
TMC and Ustraa have audiences of similar size to rivals and almost no engagement. Beardo's engagement is about 200 times TMC's (I).
Sunscreen with no white cast, a hair-loss plan, a five-blade cartridge.

5
TMC's price problem is coherence, not level
6
Where to price: two positions only
Two list prices and selling prices from Rs 180 to Rs 740 for one bottle. Confirm by a manual check, then fix before any price increase.
A fixed, honest price in the core band with a checkable claim, or a two-times premium on one named result. Every persona brand already stands in the struck-through middle.
Source: Companion document, section 1.5
